Private meetings on Discord, and owners lend their own things.

## New

- **Settings › Discord › Private meetings and events:** post them like any other (as before), post them without details ("Private LUG meeting" / "Private LUG event", no place, description or notes), or don't post them on Discord at all. Applies to announcements, threads, Discord events and reminder posts. Changing it doesn't remove anything already posted.
- **Inventory: owners lend their own things.** Someone who owns items in the inventory (the item's owner) can now lend them out and mark them returned, without the inventory permission. They see a "Lend something of yours" form with just their items, and who has them. The LUG's own things still need the inventory permission.

## Upgrading from 2.0.16

Nothing to do; there are no database changes. Private meetings keep being posted as before until you change the setting.
