More fixes for phones, and the annual report can leave out external events.

## Annual report

- **Hide external:** a button at the top of the annual report leaves External meetings and events (other groups' shows that members went to) out of every number: counts, visitors, attendance, most active members, venues and the five-year trend. "Include external" puts them back, and the choice carries over when you change the year. External events in the list are marked "External".

## Fixes

- **Long names no longer push pages off the side of the screen.** On a chapter page, a long meeting name in "Recent meetings" made the cards wider than the phone. Names are now cut short with "…" (or wrap), and the same fix applies to the other two-column layouts: meeting and event forms, recurring meetings, Settings, My Account, message wording and the annual report.
- **Annual report on a phone:** the Members and Visitors headings and the date column no longer run together.
- **Chapter page:** a small chapter no longer shows "and 0 more" under People.
