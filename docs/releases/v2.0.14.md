"I'm going" for meetings, edit and delete from an item's own page, and deleting cleans up Discord.

## New

- **Meeting RSVPs:** meeting announcements on Discord get **I'm going** and **Can't make it** buttons (answered privately; click again to take it back). The meeting page shows how many are going, who, and how many can't make it, with the same two buttons. People who said they're going get the meeting reminder as a DM (when reminder DMs are on), with a "Can't make it" button. My Account has a new "Meeting reminders" switch.
- **Edit / Delete on the meeting and event page itself**, for whoever may manage it, so you can do it from the Schedule: open the item, then Edit or Delete. (Before, those buttons were only on the old Meetings and Events lists.)

## Changed

- **Deleting a meeting or event removes everything it put elsewhere:** the announcement(s), the Discord event, its thread (if LUG Manager made it), the reminder post, the report thread and the Google Calendar entry. Afterwards you're taken back to the Schedule.

## Upgrading from 2.0.13

Migration 077 adds the `meeting_rsvps` table. Back up `lug.db` first; going back means restoring it.
