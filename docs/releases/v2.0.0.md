LUG Manager 2.0 is the biggest release yet. Members can sign in without Discord, and Discord becomes optional and much more configurable. Meetings and events come together on one Schedule page with a calendar view and one-click calendar subscriptions. Pages update live. The release also adds LEGO Fan CoLab support, inventory, treasury, colour themes, a new logo and a long list of security fixes.

## Before you upgrade

1. **Back up your data folder** (`lug.db` and `uploads/`). The database upgrades itself on first start, through migrations 044–071, and can't go back.
2. **Set your site's public address** (e.g. `https://lug.example.org`) under **Settings > Email & address**, or with the `LUG_PUBLIC_URL` environment variable, which wins when set. Emailed sign-in links and "Forgot password" emails are only sent when it's set. It also marks cookies as secure behind HTTPS.
3. **Docker:** the container now runs as an unprivileged user. It fixes the data folder's ownership on start. On Unraid, set `PUID=99` and `PGID=100` so the files belong to `nobody:users`.
4. **Reverse proxy:** allow websockets (for example, the *Websockets Support* toggle in Nginx Proxy Manager) so pages update live. Traefik and Caddy need no change. Without websockets everything still works; pages just don't update by themselves. The app refuses requests over 25 MB; if your proxy limits request sizes, 25 MB matches.
5. **If you use Discord:** older versions sent meeting and event times to Discord as if they were UTC, so they show up hours off. After upgrading, open **Settings > Discord > Repair times**. **Check** lists what's wrong; **Fix** turns those times into Discord timestamps and moves the scheduled events, without posting or notifying anyone. Shows (events) are kept by date, so their Discord events now run from the start of the first day to the end of the last.

Nothing else is required. Discord sign-in, your channels, roles and settings carry over unchanged.

## Schedule and calendars

- **Schedule:** meetings and events together on one page, as a list or a month calendar. Filter by type, dates, scope (a chapter, Group-wide or External), status, "Mine" (what you're going to, volunteering at or leading) and search. The filters are in the address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked or shared. The meetings and events tables are still there, one click away.
- **Subscribe in one click:** buttons for Apple/iPhone, Google Calendar, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365, a link to copy and a QR code for a phone, on Schedule, the dashboard, My Account and each chapter page. If your LUG's Google calendar is public, tick "This calendar is public" in Settings > Google Calendar and the Google button adds it directly, so changes show up right away.
- **Add to calendar** on every meeting and event (Google, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365 or an .ics file), and event reminder and waitlist emails carry the event as an .ics attachment.
- **Calendar feeds include their time-zone rules**, so Outlook places times correctly too.
- **"Group-wide" and "External"** replace the "LUG-wide" and "Non-LUG" labels, so they read right for Fan CoLab groups too. Nothing changes in your data or the API.

## Sign-in and accounts

- **Email and password sign-in**, with **two-factor codes** from an authenticator app and recovery codes. Admins can require two-factor for organisers or for everyone (Settings > Sign-in).
- **Emailed sign-in links** and **Forgot password** emails. Admins can make a set-password link for a member.
- **Discord sign-in is optional:** a LUG can run entirely without Discord.
- **My Account:** password and two-factor, notification choices, calendar subscriptions, signed-in devices (sign out elsewhere), download my data, delete my account.
- **Stay signed in:** sessions last 30 days from the last visit. Signing out, "sign out everywhere else", password resets and an admin's sign-out-all still end them.
- **Emails are checked:** an email a member types in themselves gets a confirmation link, and an email address can belong to one member only. When someone without an email signs in with Discord, their Discord account's verified email is filled in (turn it off under Settings > Sign-in).
- **First-run setup:** the first admin is created with a one-time link, then a checklist walks you through the rest.

## Discord, and other chat services later

- **Discord is optional:** switch it off under Settings > Features. Messages to members then go by email.
- **Times in Discord messages are Discord timestamps**, so each member sees them in their own time zone and clock format, and reminders count down ("in 2 hours").
- **Choose what's posted, per area:** announcements, threads, scheduled events, chapter copies, reminders, DMs. You can also skip it for a single meeting or event, or turn on quiet mode.
- **Your own wording for every message** (Settings > Message wording): Discord posts, messages to members and sign-in emails. Placeholders, a live preview and reset to default.
- **Activity log:** everything sent to Discord, with retry for anything that failed.
- **Pages keep working when Discord is down.** Saved channels and roles are kept.
- **Under the hood**, Discord is now one "chat provider", so Slack, Matrix and others can be added later.

## Live pages and colours

- **Live pages:** when someone changes something, everyone else's open pages update by themselves. If you're in the middle of a form, an "Updated – refresh" button waits for you instead.
- **New logo**, and an **About LUG Manager** page with the version, source code, licence and credits.
- **Colour themes:** Classic, plus Fan CoLab Creators (purple), Communities (green) and Events (blue), each in light and dark. Each member picks their own; admins set the default.
- **Dark mode**, phone-friendly pages, and installable as an app.

## LEGO Fan CoLab

- Record your recognition as a *Recognized LEGO Fan Community* and your Community Ambassador, with ambassador history.
- A yearly Fan CoLab to-do list, with a dashboard reminder.
- The year's activity summary, as a printable page or CSV.
- A public **About** page written in the app (a WYSIWYG editor with photos) and a public **Upcoming shows** page you can embed on your website.

## Running the group

- **Meetings and events:**
  - recurring meetings;
  - RSVPs with capacity and a waitlist;
  - volunteer shifts;
  - display (MOC) table requests;
  - a visitor counter;
  - kiosk check-in;
  - event photos;
  - Upcoming / Past / All lists.
- **Members:**
  - a dues ledger with automatic expiry and renewal reminders;
  - bulk edits;
  - merging duplicates;
  - guardian, consent and photo-release tracking for young members;
  - perk levels with an "Almost there" list.
- **Inventory:** storage locations with keepers, check-out and returns, due reminders, item photos and condition, pack lists per event.
- **Treasury:** income and expenses with receipts, and a treasurer role that isn't full admin.
- **Reports:** an annual report with growth and retention, and a printable per-event report.
- **Chapters:** a richer chapter page, per-chapter calendar feeds and a private personal feed.
- **Reminders** have their own page (Settings > Reminders) and work without Discord, by email.
- **Admin:** feature toggles (switch off what you don't use), daily backups including photos, CSV exports, a weekly digest email, and your own club logo.
- **Settings in the app:** the public address, the email server (with a "Send me a test email" button) and the Discord interactions key are set in Settings. An environment variable, when set, still wins and shows as "set on the server". Only secrets, start-up values and the test-copy safety switches stay environment-only.
- **Health check:** `/healthz` and a Docker `HEALTHCHECK`.

## Security

- Session tokens are stored hashed. The site has a strict Content-Security-Policy, and signed-in changes from other sites are refused.
- Emailed links only ever use `LUG_PUBLIC_URL`. A forged Host header can't redirect a password reset.
- Only admins (or the member) can change a sign-in email or a role. Discord sync only demotes roles it granted.
- Many fixes for escaping, access checks and check-in windows.

## Fixes

- **Times and dates:** times sent to Discord now carry the time zone, the server's clock follows the LUG's time zone (Settings > Calendar) for "today", "upcoming" and reminders, and meetings and events can't end before they start.
- **Settings and forms:** settings no longer clear themselves, form POSTs answer with 303, and long Discord messages are trimmed without breaking characters.
- **Performance:** caching, background publishing and faster lists. The kiosk shows a check-in within seconds.

## For developers

- **Layout:** the code is organised by area. Each integration has its own folder (`src/integrations/discord`, `google_calendar`, `email`, `ical`), and every code folder has its own `CMakeLists.txt`.
- **Tests:** unit and integration suites run against local fake Discord and Google servers, plus browser checks. `LUG_OFFLINE=1` blocks every outbound call for testing.
- **Demo:** a static demo is published to GitHub Pages.
