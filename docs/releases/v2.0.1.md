A small follow-up to 2.0.0. If you haven't upgraded yet, read the [2.0.0 notes](https://github.com/ArkLUG/lug-manager/releases/tag/v2.0.0) first; everything in "Before you upgrade" still applies.

## Changes

- **Times in Discord messages use Discord timestamps.** Meeting announcements, reminders and DMs now show each member the time in their own time zone and clock format. Reminders also count down ("in 2 hours"). Email still uses the LUG's local time. If you've edited the message wording, the new `{when_relative}` placeholder adds the countdown.
- **Repair times** (Settings > Discord) now turns the mislabelled "7:00 PM America" times in old announcements into Discord timestamps, instead of just correcting the label.
- **Reminders have their own page** (Settings > Reminders) and work without Discord. They used to live on the Discord settings page, which is hidden when Discord is switched off, so a LUG without Discord couldn't turn on RSVP, volunteer-shift or dues reminders. Those go by email when there's no Discord.
- **An email address can belong to one member only.** Before, a member could copy an admin's email onto their own record. Two members with the same email can't use it to sign in, so that locked the admin out of email sign-in.
- **Meetings and events can't end before they start.** Discord refuses those scheduled events, and calendars hide them.
- **Members get an email from Discord.** When someone without an email signs in with Discord, their Discord account's verified email is filled in, so they can also use a password, email links and email reminders. (Discord asks everyone once for permission to share the email.) It never replaces an email they have; turn it off under Settings > Sign-in.
- **New emails are confirmed.** An email a member types in themselves gets a confirmation link. Until they click it, it can't sign in or get emails. Emails set by an admin or taken from Discord count as confirmed, and so do all emails already on record.
- **Stay signed in.** Sessions last 30 days from the last visit; it used to be a hard 24 hours. Signing out, "sign out everywhere else", password resets and an admin's sign-out-all still end them.
- **The server's clock is the LUG's time zone.** "Today", "upcoming" and reminder timing used the container's time zone (UTC unless TZ is set), not the one in Settings > Calendar.
- **Requests over 25 MB are refused** before they're read into memory. Uploads stay capped at 10 MB.
- **Health check:** `/healthz` and a Docker `HEALTHCHECK`, so Unraid shows whether the app is healthy.
- **Calendar feeds include their time-zone rules (VTIMEZONE)**, so Outlook places times correctly too.
- **The kiosk screen shows a check-in within seconds** (live updates), instead of polling every 5 seconds.
- **Settings instead of environment variables.** The public address, the email server (server, user name, from address) and the Discord interactions public key are now set in the app: Settings > Email & address, with a "Send me a test email" button, and Settings > Discord matches. An environment variable, when set, still wins and shows as "set on the server". `DISCORD_REDIRECT_URI` is optional now (it defaults to the public address + `/auth/callback`).
- Housekeeping: one htmx version everywhere.

## Upgrading from 2.0.0

Nothing to do: migration 071 runs by itself. If you limit request sizes at your reverse proxy, 25 MB matches the app.

What stays an environment variable, and why:
- **Secrets:** the Discord client secret and bot token, and the email server's password (`LUG_SMTP_PASSWORD`). The app never shows or stores them.
- **Needed before the app can read its settings:** the port, the database path and the templates folder (`LUG_PORT`, `LUG_DB_PATH`, `LUG_TEMPLATES_DIR`).
- **First sign-in:** `BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_DISCORD_ID`, and the Discord client ID that sign-in starts with.
- **Safety switches for test copies:** `LUG_OFFLINE` and `LUG_DOTENV`. They must not be changeable from a running site.
