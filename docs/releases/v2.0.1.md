A small follow-up to 2.0.0, about dues. If you haven't upgraded to 2.0 yet, read the [2.0.0 notes](https://github.com/ArkLUG/lug-manager/releases/tag/v2.0.0) first.

## Dues

- **Settings > Dues:** set the yearly amount, the month your dues year ends (December means the calendar year), and whether part-year payments are prorated.
- **Recording a payment fills itself in.** On a member's Dues panel, "Record a payment" suggests the amount and the "covers until" date for the payment date:
  - a full year: the full amount, to the end of the dues year;
  - joining part-way through (with proration on): the months left, counting the month they join, rounded up to whole dollars, e.g. $20 a year, joining in July: $10;
  - already paid for this year: the next full year.
  You can change both before saving.
- **Record past payments:** members marked paid before 2.0 have no payment on record, so their dues weren't in the Treasury or annual report totals. Settings > Dues can record one payment each (an amount and date you choose), covering to their current paid-until date, which doesn't change.
