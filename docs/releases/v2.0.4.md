Money, email and a design fix.

## Dues and treasury

- **Grace period for dues** (Settings > Dues): members stay counted as paid for the number of days you choose after their dues run out, before they're marked unpaid. Their Dues panel says "Grace period" with the last day, and the members list shows "Grace".
- **Edit dues payments:** admins and treasurers can now edit a payment on a member's Dues panel (date, amount, method, covers until, note). If a payment set the member's paid-until date, editing or deleting it moves paid-until to match the remaining payments.
- **Edit treasury entries:** change the date, type, amount, category, event or description, and replace or remove the receipt. Every change is recorded in the audit log with what changed.
- **Treasury visibility** (new Settings > Treasury page): choose who can see the Overview (totals and breakdowns), the Ledger, the Receipts, and who paid dues: admins and treasurers only (the default, as before), also chapter leads and moderators, or all members. Only admins and treasurers can change anything. This lets you share how the money is doing with members without showing details.
- **Treasury has Overview and Ledger tabs.**

## Email

- **Set up email completely in the app** (Settings > Email & address): pick your provider (Gmail / Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Outlook.com, iCloud, Fastmail, Zoho, SendGrid, Mailgun, Amazon SES, or other) to fill in the server, then enter the user name, **password** and the address to send as.
- The password is stored encrypted and never shown again. Its key is a new file, `data/secret.key`, created automatically; copy it along if you move to a new server.
- **"Send me a test email"** now sends straight away and shows the mail server's error if it fails.
- The `LUG_SMTP_*` environment variables still work and win when set.
