## New

- **Settings › Perk levels › "Virtual meetings count toward perk levels":** when ticked, joining a meeting online counts the same as being there in person for perk levels, the attendance overview's tiers, the dashboard ("Your attendance", "Almost at the next perk level") and perk Discord roles. Off by default, so only in-person meetings count, as before. Attendance lists still show who was virtual, and "verified member" status (for privacy sharing) stays in person only. Changing it is admin-only and recorded in the audit log.
