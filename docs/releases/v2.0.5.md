Buttons on reminders, show hours, inventory owners, a logo editor, labelled forms and a tidier sidebar.

## New

- **Buttons on Discord reminder DMs:** View event / My dues, **Can't make it** (gives up your RSVP or volunteer shift; the waitlist moves up), **Remind me later**, and **Don't remind me** (with Undo). The action buttons need Discord's Interactions Endpoint set up (Settings > Discord matches); without it, DMs get the link buttons only.
- **Show hours:** each event has an Hours panel: setup, open to the public and teardown times, several a day if needed, and "same public hours every day". The Discord event, the calendar feeds and the public Shows page use the public hours; setup and teardown appear in members' calendars and the Schedule, and are check-in days. Events without hours work as before (all-day dates).
- **Inventory owners:** items and storage locations belong to the LUG or to a member (e.g. someone's trailer, or their tables kept in the LUG's unit), separately from where they're kept and who looks after them. Filter by Mine / The LUG's; the CSV has an owner column; My Account lists your things the LUG keeps.
- **Logo editor** (Settings > Branding): size, drag to position, fit or fill, background colour (picker or hex) or transparent, with previews. The original is kept so the logo can be edited again.

## Easier to use

- **Sidebar:** grouped under Community and Admin, with one **Settings** link to a page that lists every setting, grouped.
- **Every form field has a label** (some only had a placeholder, or nothing).
- **Discord interactions endpoint hardened:** requests older than 5 minutes are refused (replay), bodies over 16 KB get 413, an address that keeps failing the signature check gets 429 (genuine requests from Discord are never refused by this), and signed requests of a kind we don't handle get 400.
- Unknown addresses show a "Page not found" page instead of a server error.
