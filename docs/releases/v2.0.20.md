Money by chapter.

## New

- **Dues split between chapter and LUG:** Settings › Dues has "Chapter's share of each payment" (default 50%). That part of each dues payment counts toward the member's chapter, the rest toward the LUG; members without a chapter put it all toward the LUG. Each payment keeps the chapter and the share it was recorded with, so changing the setting, or someone moving chapter, never changes past payments.
- **Income and expenses for a chapter:** recording or editing an entry on the Treasury Ledger has a "For" choice: the whole LUG or a chapter.
- **Treasury › Overview › By chapter:** for the LUG and each chapter, its dues, other income, expenses, net for the year and balance at the end of the year. They add up to the LUG-wide figures, which don't change. The Ledger shows each dues payment's chapter share and what each entry is for; the CSV has a Chapter column.

## Upgrading from 2.0.19

Migration 078. Existing dues payments are counted toward each member's chapter as it is now, at 50%; existing income and expenses count as the whole LUG's. The LUG-wide totals stay exactly the same. Back up `lug.db` first; going back means restoring it.
