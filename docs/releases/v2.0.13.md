Guardians are members, chapters show on member pages, and the attendance overview sorts by perk tier.

## Changed

- **Guardian & consent** is for KFOL and TFOL members only: the section shows on the member form once the age range is KFOL or TFOL, and saving an adult clears it. The guardian is now **another member of the club**, picked from the members list (adults only, never the member themselves); their phone and email come from their own record, and the member page links to them. Guardians typed in by name before are linked automatically when exactly one adult member has that name; otherwise the old details stay, marked "not linked to a member yet", until someone picks the member.
- **Attendance overview:** the Tier column sorts (highest tier first, then by name), for the year shown.

## Fixed

- The member page showed "Chapter" with nothing after it; it shows the chapter's name.

## Upgrading from 2.0.12

Migration 076 adds `members.guardian_member_id` and links existing guardians by name where it's unambiguous. Back up `lug.db` first; going back to 2.0.12 means restoring it.
