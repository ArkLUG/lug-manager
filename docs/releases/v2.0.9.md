Roles and permissions: choose what moderators, chapter leads and members may do.

## New

- **Settings › Roles and permissions:** a grid of permissions by role (Moderator, Chapter lead, Member) with a checkbox each: see contact details and dues status, add and edit members, download the members list, link Discord accounts, record dues, manage the inventory, manage every meeting and event, run build challenges, LEGO Fan CoLab, attendance overview, annual report, audit log. Admins can always do everything. The sidebar shows each person what they may open.
- **Moderator from Discord:** Settings › Discord roles has a Moderator row, so a Discord role can make someone a moderator on sign-in and member sync.

## Defaults

Nothing changes until you change it: moderators and chapter leads keep exactly what they could do before, and everything that was admin-only stays admin-only. A permission not ticked is denied.

## Safety

- Only admins can open Roles and permissions, change roles or change settings, so no permission can be used to raise your own access. Every change to the grid is in the audit log.
- Anyone who isn't an admin now only adds plain members (before, a chapter lead could create a member as a Moderator or Chapter lead).
- The API rejects role mappings to unknown roles.

## Upgrading from 2.0.8

Migration 075 adds the `role_permissions` table with the defaults above. Back up `lug.db` first: going back to 2.0.8 means restoring that backup.
