Welcome to LUG Manager

Here's everything you need to know for your role as {{role_label}}.

Interactive tour

Take a guided walkthrough that navigates you through each page and highlights key features.

Getting started (everyone)
1
Edit your profile — Click the yellow "Edit my profile" button on the dashboard or members page. Add your name, contact info, and choose your privacy settings for each field.
2
Subscribe to the calendar — Schedule shows meetings and events together, as a list or a month calendar, with filters (type, dates, scope, status, "Mine", search). "Add the whole schedule to your calendar" there (or under My Account, or on a chapter page for just that chapter) has one-click buttons for Apple/iPhone, Google Calendar, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365, a link to copy and a QR code for your phone. Google Calendar can take up to a day to show changes, unless an admin has made the LUG's Google calendar public and ticked "This calendar is public" in Settings > Google Calendar: then the Google button adds that calendar, which updates right away. Each meeting and event also has "Add to calendar" for just that one, and reminder emails come with it attached.
3
Browse members — Go to the Members page to see who's in the LUG. Click "View" on any member to see their shared contact info.
4
Privacy settings — You control who sees your contact info. Set each field (email, phone, address, birthday, Discord) to "Don't share", "Verified members only", or "All members". Verified = has attended in person or paid dues.
5
RSVP to events — Open an event and use the RSVP panel. If the event is full you join the waitlist and move up automatically when a spot opens. RSVPs close at the sign-up deadline.
6
Bring a display — For public shows that take display requests, open the event and use "Request display space" to register your MOC with its size (inches) and power needs. The event lead approves it and assigns a table.
7
Dark mode — Use the sun / monitor / moon buttons at the bottom of the sidebar to pick Light, System (follows your device) or Dark.
Colours — Pick a colour theme under the Theme buttons (or My Account > Colours): Classic, or Fan CoLab Creators (purple), Communities (green) or Events (blue), in the colours of the LEGO Fan CoLab site. It follows you to your other devices. Admins set the default for everyone else in Settings > Branding.
8
My Account — "My Account" at the bottom of the sidebar is where you choose which Discord DMs you get (event and shift reminders, waitlist spots, dues reminders), see where you're signed in, download a copy of your data, or delete your account. Password & two-factor (on My Account) lets you set a password to sign in with your email, and turn on two-factor: after signing in you also enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (keep the recovery codes somewhere safe). Signed-in devices: it lists where you're signed in; "Sign out everywhere else" logs out other devices. You stay signed in on a device while you keep using it; after 30 days without a visit you sign in again. If your LUG has the weekly digest turned on, you also get a Monday-morning summary of your week; turn it off there like any other message. Reminder DMs on Discord come with buttons: open the event (or your dues), Can't make it to give up your RSVP or volunteer shift, Remind me later, and Don't remind me (with an Undo).
9
Private calendar feed — On the dashboard's calendar card, "Get my private feed" gives you a personal link that includes private meetings and events. Keep it to yourself.
10
Install the app — In your browser's menu choose "Install" / "Add to Home Screen" to use LUG Manager like an app.
Live pages — Pages update by themselves when someone else changes something (a new RSVP, a check-in, an edited event). If you're filling in a form, nothing is swapped under you: an "Updated – refresh" button appears instead, so finish what you're doing and click it.
11
Event photos — Open an event and use "Add a photo" in the Photos panel. Photos are only visible to signed-in members, and location data is removed. You can delete your own photos.
12
Build challenges — Under Build Challenges, enter one build per challenge while entries are open, then vote for a favourite (not your own). Votes are revealed a week after entries close.
13
Inventory — The Inventory page lists the LUG's own gear, which storage location (storage unit, trailer, someone's garage) each item is kept at, and anything checked out to you. If you look after a location, it's shown at the top. Organisers can give items a photo and a condition, and build a pack list on each event page (what to bring, where it's kept), with a printable checklist for loading up. {{leads_and_mods}} and admins add items, check them out to members and mark them returned.
Event lead guide
1
What is an event lead? — Any member can be assigned as the lead for a specific event. This gives you management permissions for that one event only, even if you're not an event manager or chapter lead.
2
Your powers — As event lead you can edit the event, take attendance, generate QR check-in codes, and publish reports to Discord — the same capabilities as an event manager, but scoped to your assigned event.
3
How you're assigned — An admin{{#f_chapters}}, chapter lead, or event manager{{/f_chapters}}{{^f_chapters}} or moderator{{/f_chapters}} selects you from the "Event Lead" dropdown when creating or editing an event. You'll see the Edit/Attendance/QR buttons on events where you're the lead.

Note: Event lead permissions apply only to the specific event(s) you're assigned to, not to all events.

Event manager guide
1
Create meetings — Go to Meetings and click "New Meeting". Fill in the title, location and date/time{{#f_chapters}}, and select your chapter{{/f_chapters}}. Virtual meetings hide the location and show a Discord voice channel picker instead.
2
Create events — Go to Events and click "New Event". Events are date-based (no times). Choose scope: {{#f_chapters}}Chapter, {{/f_chapters}}Group-wide, or External. External events don't have status tracking. You can assign an event lead to delegate management of the event.
3
Take attendance — Click "View" on a meeting or event to open the detail modal. The attendance panel at the bottom lets you search and add members. Use the multi-select dropdown to check in multiple people at once.
4
QR code check-in — In the detail modal, click "Show QR Code" to generate a scannable check-in link. Attendees scan it on their phone and can check in via Discord, by finding their name, or by entering their info as a new member.
5
Assign event leads — When creating or editing an event, use the "Event Lead" dropdown to assign a member as lead. That person gains edit, attendance, and QR permissions for that specific event.
6
Publish reports — After a meeting or event, click "Post report to Discord" to post an attendance summary to the Discord forum channel.
7
Check-in kiosk — Next to "Show QR Code", "Kiosk" opens a full-screen page for a tablet or projector at the venue with a big QR code, a live count and the latest arrivals.
8
Recurring meetings — Meetings → "Recurring" sets up a schedule (e.g. 2nd Tuesday monthly) and creates the meetings and their announcements about 45 days ahead. Edit or cancel any single meeting as usual; "Stop series" removes the future ones.
9
Manage RSVPs and displays — As a manager you see everyone's RSVPs and display requests on the event page. Open or close display requests, approve or decline them, assign tables and export a CSV.

{{lead_guide_title}}
1
Manage members — You can add new members, edit existing ones, and manage dues (mark paid/unpaid). Go to the Members page — you'll see Edit, Dues, and Add Member buttons.
2
View all PII — You can see all member contact info regardless of their privacy settings. This is for running the LUG.
3
Everything event managers can do — You also have all event manager capabilities: create/edit meetings and events, take attendance, generate QR codes, and publish reports.
4
Dues history — Open a member and use "Record a payment" to log dues with amount, method and covers-until date. Set the yearly amount and when the dues year ends under Settings > Dues: the form then fills in the amount and covers-until date, prorated for someone joining part-way through (you can change both before saving). The dashboard lists dues expiring in the next 30 days; lapsed dues are marked unpaid automatically.
5
Bulk changes — Tick members in the Members table to mark them paid/unpaid, {{#f_chapters}}move them to a chapter, {{/f_chapters}}or set their age range in one go. Use CSV to export the list.

Note: Only admins can change member roles, delete members, or change {{#f_chapters}}chapter {{/f_chapters}}settings.

Admin guide
1
Pick your features — Settings > Features switches off anything your LUG doesn't use (for example Chapters, Dues or the Treasury). Switched-off features vanish from menus and pages but keep their data. Then configure Discord — go to Settings to set your Guild ID, announcement channels, forum channels, and timezone. The bot needs to be invited to your server with proper permissions first.
2
Map Discord roles — Go to Settings > Role Mappings to map Discord roles to admin or member LUG roles. Members of your Discord server are auto-provisioned when they log in based on these mappings. Mapped roles follow Discord (losing the Discord role removes it); roles you set by hand on a member are never lowered by sync.
3 {{#f_chapters}}
Set up chapters — Go to Chapters to create sub-groups. Assign leads (who get synced Discord roles) and event managers. Each chapter gets its own announcement channel.
{{/f_chapters}} {{^f_chapters}}
Pick your organisers — Make trusted members Moderators (Members > Edit > Role). They can add and edit members, manage dues, and run meetings and events.
{{/f_chapters}}
4
Configure perk levels — Go to Perk Levels to define attendance tiers (e.g. Bronze, Silver, Gold) with meeting/event requirements, dues prerequisites, and an optional Discord role.
5
Monitor activity — Use the Attendance Overview to see who's attending, and the Audit Log to track all actions taken by admins and managers.
6
Sync members — In Settings, use "Sync Now" to pull all Discord guild members into the system. This runs automatically every 6 hours. Use "Sync All to Discord" to push meetings/events to Discord.
7
What's posted to Discord — Settings > Discord chooses what each new meeting and event gets (announcement, thread, chapter copy, Discord event, "updated" notes), whether roles are pinged, whether members get DMs (otherwise email), and nickname handling. Each meeting and event form has "Discord: leave out parts" for exceptions, and "Don't post to Discord at all". Quiet mode posts nothing new while you import or tidy up. The Activity log shows every post, edit and failure, with Retry. Message wording (Settings) changes the text of every message, with a live preview. Switch Discord off completely under Settings > Features.
9
Reminders — Settings > Reminders: reminders before meetings and events (posted on Discord when it's on), to members who RSVP'd and to volunteers, and before dues run out. Members get theirs as a Discord DM, or by email when they're not on Discord.
8
Backups — Settings > Backups takes a daily snapshot of the database (keeping the newest 14 by default), lets you back up on demand, and download copies to keep off the server.
9
Exports — The Attendance Overview and Audit Log have CSV export buttons. Exports are recorded in the audit log.
10
Treasury — Record table fees, supplies, donations and other money in Treasury; dues recorded on member records are included automatically. Link an entry to an event to see it on that event's report. Each year has totals, an opening/closing balance and a CSV export. Attach a receipt photo or PDF to any entry. To let someone who isn't an admin keep the books, edit their member record and tick "Treasurer".
12
Merge duplicates — If someone ends up with two member records (e.g. added by hand, then signed in with Discord), use Members > Merge. Preview shows what moves; the duplicate is then deleted.
13
Public shows page — Turn on the public shows page under Settings > Public pages to publish upcoming events at /shows for anyone (private and cancelled events are left out). Copy the embed snippet to put the list on your website. Visitors can tap "I plan to come"; the count shows on the event page and report to help plan.
14
Sign-in and members without Discord — Give members an email address on their record (one per person). Members who sign in with Discord get their Discord account's verified email filled in automatically if their record has none (Settings > Sign-in can turn that off). An email a member types in themselves is confirmed first: they get a link, and until they click it the address can't sign in or get emails. From their member page, "Make a set-password link" gives you a link to send them (24 hours, works once), or "Email them…" sends it if the server has email set up. Members can also set a password themselves under My Account, use "Forgot it?", or "Email me a link". Settings > Sign-in turns password sign-in and emailed links on or off and can require two-factor for organisers or everyone; "Reset two-factor" on a member page helps someone who lost their phone and codes. Each email has an unsubscribe link that works without signing in.
15
LEGO Fan CoLab — LEGO Fan CoLab replaced the LEGO Ambassador Network in 2026; recognized LUGs are now Recognized LEGO Fan Communities, and groups had to reapply. If yours is recognized, record it and your Community Ambassador in Settings > Setup (step 5) so reports and the public shows page say so. Fan CoLab's guidelines ask for an organised group of adult fans that meets regularly, a named Community Ambassador who isn't also the president/chair, and that you tell the Fan CoLab team about leadership or ambassador changes. The LEGO Fan CoLab page (sidebar) keeps the Community Ambassador history (changing the ambassador starts a new term; add earlier ones by hand), a yearly to-do list that admins see on the dashboard until it's done, and the year's activity summary as a printable page or CSV for when Fan CoLab asks what you've been up to. Check fancolab.lego.com for the current rules.
16
About page — Settings > Public pages lets admins write a public page about the group at /about: who you are, when you meet, how to join. The editor works like a word processor (headings, bold, lists, quotes, links and photos), and pasting from a document keeps that formatting. Tick "Show the page to everyone" to publish it. It can also show the past twelve months in numbers. Photos removed from the text are deleted when you save.
11
Build challenges — Under Build Challenges, create a theme with an entry window. Members get a week after entries close to vote; then "Announce winner on Discord" posts the result to the LUG channel.
