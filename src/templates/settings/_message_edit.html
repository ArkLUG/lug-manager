How the wording works
  • A placeholder in curly brackets is replaced by its value.
  • A line whose placeholders are all empty is left out (so "Location: …" disappears when there's no location).
  • Put optional bits in double square brackets, like [[ at {location}]]: they're left out when a placeholder in them is empty.
    • {{^is_email_only}}
  • **bold** and *italic* work in Discord{{#is_dm}}; in emails they're turned into plain text{{/is_dm}}.
    • {{/is_email_only}}
  • To write a curly bracket itself, double it.
