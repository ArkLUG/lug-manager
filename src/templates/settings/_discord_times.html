Repair times on Discord

Before October 2026, LUG Manager's Docker image had no time-zone data, so it sent meeting and event times to Discord as if they were UTC ({{timezone}} was ignored). Discord's scheduled events show those as hours early. Meeting announcements have the right hours but say "7:00 PM America" instead of a time zone. Google Calendar and the calendar feed were never affected.

Check only reads from Discord. Fix edits the wrong ones in place: the scheduled event's start and end, and the announcement's times, which become Discord timestamps that every member sees in their own time zone (as new announcements do). It doesn't post anything new, ping anyone, rename threads or touch Google Calendar, so members aren't notified. Events that have already started can't be moved on Discord, so they're left as they are.
